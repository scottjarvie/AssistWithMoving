# Cross-site design playbook

Scott, 2026-11-04. Supplied in chat on 2026-11-05. Text descriptions govern; pattern screenshots are not present on oldwin.

## §2 Principles

1 No cheating — never solve a design problem by removing/hiding information or capability.
2 Quick-read overviews; depth one labelled step away (tabs, contents list, side panel, "behind the number" drawer); no mega pages.
3 Icons for metadata (you vs which AI, kind, source) with hover/tap details.
4 Compact icon buttons; big buttons are a regression.
5 Keep the record's image slot (photo or domain illustration).
6 Evidence and knowledge first; reminders secondary unless used.
7 Show only what the person actually uses.
8 Collections keep everything (table/list/grid, filters, sort, search, bulk select, density, columns).
9 Phone and desktop may differ; same data and capability.
10 Judges advise; Scott decides.

## §3 Patterns

### 3.1 Record

Dossier card + one evidence stream. Desktop: left card (image slot, identity, compact actions: add entry with kind menu, camera, write recap, ask AI, ⋯; facts each with "from its entry" source link; connections; domain aside modules). Right: one dated stream (recap/insights pinned, entries newest first with kind icon + You/AI chip, kind filter chips, "N earlier entries · Show all"). Phone: compact card then stream. Facts once per page. Not chosen: two-pane rail, bento.

### 3.2 Sections

Sticky tab strip with icon + count, "All N" menu pinned right; phone strip scrolls sideways with "All N" always visible + two-column Contents list on overview; one-line tab names. Not chosen: side index, accordion.

### 3.3 Collections

Saved views as tabs (only real views + All + real built-in states; edited view: dot + Discard / Save as view). Toolbar: search, + Filter, Group ▾, Sort ▾, Table/List/Grid, Density, Columns. Removable filter chips; + Filter lists every facet with counts; Clear all. Grouped table with summary chips, add row, footer "4 of 19 shown · 15 hidden by this view · 1 archived hidden · Show". Grid = visual card; List = clean list. Phone: view pills, search + Filter, Group, Sort, Options; quick filter sheet with counts (no nested menus).

### 3.4 Activity

One lane per entry kind used + dated list; person marks filled, AI accent, routine hollow, superseded struck; measurements print values; All/You/AI; 3 mo · Year · All; drag for range; phone: lane switcher then list.

### 3.5 Home

Desk of labelled trays (folder tab, one count, top few items, one named link): Ready for you, This week, Recently touched (image slots), With your AI, AI suggestions, Library + site equivalents; evidence-first; ~7 trays max. Not chosen: daybook, ask-first brief.

### 3.6 Stats

Instrument panel (hairline grid of labelled small charts/tables); every number opens a "behind the number" panel of matching records with "Open as a view"/"Save view"; Ledger acceptable alternative; never fake a trend.

### 3.7 Admin

Desktop Ledger tables + sticky tabs, rows open in place, Instrument small charts as headers, read-only account drawer (dangerous actions behind named confirmation); phone instrument panels, drawer as sheet.

### 3.8 Settings AI

Permissions matrix (connections × abilities, summary sentence per row, ring on AI requests, pencil editor, person-only actions table); phone: card per connection. Revoke confirms; AI can't widen its own access; never invent capabilities.

### 3.9 Settings data

Desktop: table of every way data can leave (what, who, how long, your control); phone: dossier + stream of ways out.

### 3.10 Learning

Study: focused session; reading: field guide with sidenote sources + provenance card.

### 3.11 Queue

List = §3.3 (Needs you · Waiting on AI · AI working · Done this week · Everything; grouped by state; "Needs you" is a flag; answer in the row). Item: desktop thread; phone answer-first.

## §4 Skeleton and flair

If it changes where things are or what you can do → skeleton (match); how it looks or what it's called → flair (Moving's own).

## §5 Heuristics

Extend nearest decided pattern; density for power pages, calm for daily; tables compare, cards recognise; every number links to records; doing → focused surface, reading → calm column; AI work attributed; phones not shrunk desktops; ties → reuse decided pattern; ask Scott only for capability/access changes; don't invent capabilities.

## §7 Anti-patterns

Unused reminders as hero; cut-off tab text; big buttons; lost image slot; mega overviews; accordion Expand all; sideways-scroll-only sections; repeated facts; invented views/capabilities/fake trends; SaaS bento grids; judges' density preference overriding Scott.

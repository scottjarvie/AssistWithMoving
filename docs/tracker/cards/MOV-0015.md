## Why this exists

People should be able to reach help and understand that Moving belongs to an
Assist family without losing Moving's distinct product identity or landing on
invented destinations.

## Current truth

The public header/footer provides Moving-specific product, AI, API, MCP, PCS,
claims, privacy, and updates navigation. No shared family-project row or public
Support Desk destination is present. Product-scoped error copy says to contact
support, but the audit found no verified public support route or shared family
URL set. Admin source includes internal support search, which is not a person’s
support channel.

**Priority:** Later until destinations are verified. **Change class:** product
navigation plus external portfolio dependency.

## Next safe action

Obtain the canonical current family destinations and shared Support Desk URL,
then add a compact family row and Support link to public/product chrome and
recovery copy. Label unavailable family destinations Coming soon rather than
creating dead links.

## Constraints

- Do not invent, register, redirect, or change domains.
- Preserve MovingManifest's name, design, and primary navigation hierarchy.
- Support must be reachable without an internal tracker account.
- UI/navigation changes use normal safeguards.

## Completion evidence

- Every family and Support URL has an owner-provided canonical source and a
  successful live destination or explicit Coming soon presentation.
- Public footer, signed-in shell, `/me`, and error recovery expose the same
  reachable Support destination without crowding phone navigation.
- Link crawl, keyboard/focus, responsive, full CI, and live checks pass.
- Copy clearly distinguishes product support from internal admin search.

## Unknown or access limitation

Canonical family-row membership and Support Desk URL were not available in the
repository or audit task. This Card should remain Backlog until those inputs are
verified.

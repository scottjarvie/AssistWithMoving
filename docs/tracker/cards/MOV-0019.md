## Why this exists

Move records can reveal addresses, dates, valuables, serial numbers, room
layouts, identity documents, and family photos. People need plain operational
guidance about safe capture and sharing in addition to backend controls.

## Current truth

The public privacy page names the sensitive data categories, private originals,
scoped access, redacted packet views, and user-controlled sharing. It also says
it is an overview and that a formal legal privacy policy has not been reviewed.
Public FAQ/features make additional access and AI claims, while no verified
public Support destination exists. Product source has disclosure and redaction
controls, but authenticated behavior was not tested.

**Priority:** Next. **Change class:** documentation/trust gap with real product
acceptance dependencies.

## Next safe action

Reconcile privacy copy with the actual access, storage, AI, export, retention,
deletion, and support behavior; add concise moving-specific safety guidance for
photos/documents/addresses and external shares; obtain the appropriate owner or
legal review before changing the page's review status.

## Constraints

- Do not invent legal compliance, encryption guarantees, retention periods,
  breach processes, or provider behavior.
- Keep operational safety guidance distinct from formal legal terms.
- Do not advise users to place secrets in AI prompts, screenshots, or trackers.
- Docs and any linked product-control changes use normal safeguards unless an
  exact pure state-only package is separately approved by the helper.

## Completion evidence

- Every privacy/safety statement maps to tested product/provider behavior or is
  explicitly labeled policy/coming soon.
- Guidance covers address/date minimization, identity documents, children/family
  photos, serials/values, AI scope, public/action shares, revocation, and support.
- Owner/legal review provenance and effective date are recorded without
  overstating the review.
- Link, responsive, accessibility, full CI/deploy, and live copy checks pass.

## Unknown or access limitation

No legal reviewer, production retention configuration, provider dashboard, or
incident/support process was available in this audit.

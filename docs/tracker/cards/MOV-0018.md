## Why this exists

Public product, privacy, AI, API, MCP, FAQ, and update pages should load quickly
and avoid unnecessary identity/data vendor requests before a person chooses to
sign in.

## Current truth

`src/app/layout.tsx` wraps every route in Clerk and the Convex client provider
when public environment keys exist. A dated performance audit measured 765 KB
of decoded Clerk JavaScript plus blocking identity requests and telemetry on
marketing pages and proposed moving providers into protected route groups. The
current root source still contains the global wrappers. This audit did not
repeat a complete network/decoded-bundle trace, so the historical byte count is
evidence to refresh, not a current measurement.

**Priority:** Next. **Change class:** product performance/privacy architecture.

## Next safe action

Re-measure public requests and bundles on current production, inventory every
route-group hook, then relocate Clerk/Convex providers to the smallest auth,
product, MCP-connect, or share boundary that actually needs them.

## Constraints

- Do not break sign-in, OAuth connect, public share viewing, or runtime fallback
  behavior when credentials are absent.
- Preserve server-side proxy protection independently of client providers.
- Use e2e network assertions, not a unit-only claim about vendor requests.
- Layout/provider/test changes require normal safeguards.

## Completion evidence

- `/`, `/faq`, `/ai`, `/api`, `/mcp/guide`, `/privacy`, and `/updates` make zero
  Clerk/identity telemetry and zero Convex WebSocket requests before sign-in.
- Current decoded JavaScript and load metrics are recorded before/after; public
  pages meet a reviewed budget without regressing accessibility or metadata.
- Auth, OAuth connect, protected app, and public share scenarios still work in
  configured and missing-env builds.
- Full CI, network e2e, normal deploy, and live performance proof pass.

## Unknown or access limitation

The audit verified current provider placement and historical measurements but
did not capture a fresh production waterfall after closing the browser session.

## Why this exists

A person may direct their own AI to contact a mover, compare a marketplace,
prepare a claim, or do other outside work. Moving may eventually pass selected
context and approved intent to that AI and preserve the receipt, while never
claiming that Moving itself contacted, bought, booked, signed, paid, published,
or otherwise acted outside the product.

## Current truth

Scoped keys, OAuth/client identity, Queue instructions, audit fields, evidence,
and source-backed results are partial foundations. No complete product contract
currently binds chosen AI, selected data, external-action category and intent,
specific approval, duration, revocation, handoff time, result/failure/return,
and human review. `MOV-WO-010` deliberately excludes this outside-world layer
and limits `MOV-0023` to Moving's product grants.

## Next safe action

After `MOV-WO-010` proves narrow product grants, define a separate bounded Work
Order for external-action categories, approved data/intent packets, receipts,
failure/return states, review, retention, and revocation. Start with one real
Moving scenario rather than a generic action system.

## Constraints

- Moving never independently performs the outside action.
- A person remains free to direct their AI outside Moving; Moving governs only
  whether its data/tools participate and what receipt it preserves.
- Product read/write/Queue authority never implies an external-action grant.
- Queue text, AI inference, prior conversation, or another actor's grant cannot
  create or widen outside-action authority.
- No provider credentials, messaging, offers, purchases, bookings, signatures,
  payments, publishing, real-user data, or production integration under this
  backlog Card.

## Completion evidence

- One scenario records actor/AI, selected data, action category and intent,
  approval, duration/revocation, handoff time, result/failure/return, and review.
- Missing or revoked authority refuses the handoff without policing the person's
  external conversation.
- Product copy and activity history say the chosen AI acted from an authorized
  handoff, never that Moving itself took the outside action.
- Provider, privacy, retention, failure, recovery, cleanup, release, and
  independent-audit evidence exist before Current claims.

## Unknown or access limitation

No external-action provider or production workflow was inspected or changed.

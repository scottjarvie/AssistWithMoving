## Why this exists

When a person or AI changes a move, the household must be able to understand
who acted, what changed, when, under which authority, and how to review or undo
the result.

## Current truth

Moving has `auditLogs`, human/agent/API-key actor types, selected object and move
activity views, AI review states, plan-operation inverses, recent agent batches,
and many audited mutations. The Project Philosophy correctly labels this a
partial foundation: coverage, before/after detail, evidence links, and readable
AI attribution are not universal. No authenticated history view was tested.

**Priority:** Next. **Change class:** product, audit-contract, and verification
gap.

## Next safe action

Inventory every user-visible write across browser, REST, hosted MCP, API-key
MCP, background job, admin, and share-link actions; close missing audit events
and present one readable activity model in move/object views and `/me`.

## Constraints

- Preserve actor identity, authority, scope, timestamp, source, and request/id
  linkage without logging secrets or sensitive payloads.
- Do not imply an undo exists unless its inverse and conflict behavior are
  tested.
- Keep operational/admin audit separate from household-private detail.
- Schema/query/UI changes use normal safeguards and bounded retention.

## Completion evidence

- A write-surface matrix maps every mutation/tool/action to an audit event,
  readable label, affected record, actor type, authority, and evidence link.
- Marked person, API-key AI, OAuth AI, background, share, and admin scenarios
  show correct attribution and no secret/private-data leakage.
- History is understandable on phone and desktop, and documented reversible
  actions undo safely while non-reversible actions say so.
- Full CI, retention/query-cost review, live fixture proof, and independent
  audit pass.

## Unknown or access limitation

This audit verified representative source and the documented partial status,
not exhaustive mutation coverage or live activity accuracy.

## Why this exists

Moving's scoped connection, Queue, photo delivery, and complete-result tools are
built and deployed. A realistic synthetic move should now prove whether those
parts actually help a person make decisions across rooms and possessions and
arrive with a usable first-week continuity plan.

## Outcome

A chosen AI reads only one approved move, works through photos and belongings,
records keep/sell/donate/store/pack questions and evidence, and saves a reviewable
first-week plan. Scott can correct, continue, or revoke it from the product.

## Definition of done

Recorded in MOV-WO-011. A live named-client receipt remains separate from local
scenario completion and uses only a marked synthetic production identity.

## Current truth

MOV-WO-010 is built and deployed with grants, fifteen tools, Queue work,
budgeted photo bytes, settings, and a lifecycle harness. The named-client run
still needs a human-approved marked production identity.

## Next safe action

Complete that existing human-gated lifecycle, then run MOV-WO-011 against a
removable synthetic move.

## Constraints

No real household records, movers, sales, disposal, payment, booking, message,
permanent delete, or provider/security change.

## Completion evidence

A dated receipt covers room/item/photo work, possessions decisions, the
first-week plan, UI review, revoke, reconnect, and zero-residue cleanup.

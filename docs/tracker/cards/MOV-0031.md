## Why this exists

The first live Queue-to-canonical-MCP acceptance loop proved that a chosen AI
could read the correct private move and handoff, then save a source-backed
complete result. The normal Move overview showed that result, but the Queue
handoff did not render its advertised link. The same single-owner first run
defaulted to Everyone's Queue and disabled the handoff composer until the person
manually selected My Queue.

## Current truth

PR `#186` released the repair. The complete planning result attaches to the
exact owner-scoped Queue item without claiming or completing it, records
attributable Queue activity, and renders linked work plus a direct Saved work
path in the handoff. A move with no other Queue owner defaults to My Queue;
multi-owner managers retain the aggregate Everyone's Queue default. The live
retained-account rerun verified the Queue and Move overview against production,
then permanently removed the marked move and every linked record.

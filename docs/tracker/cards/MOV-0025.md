## Why this exists

The real backend should lead to a clear Moving-specific experience, not force
Claude Design to reverse-engineer placeholder cards or copy another Assist site.

## Current truth

`docs/planning/queue-foundation-and-design-handoff.md` now defines exact data,
states, actions, authority, failures, activity, legacy capture mapping, system
states, existing component seams, and validation scenarios. The current UI at
`/app/queue` and `/app/moves/{moveId}/queue` remains capture-oriented and uses
To do/Working/Review/Done groupings. Live `/queue` was a verified 404 on
2026-08-09. This backend task intentionally did not choose the final route
composition, cards, navigation, or visual design.

**Priority:** Next, after the foundation deploys. **Change class:** Claude Design
plus frontend product implementation and browser verification.

## Next safe action

Give Claude Design the handoff and representative synthetic state fixtures.
Design the canonical route and capture coexistence around Moving's field-desk,
route-note identity, then implement through a separate protected UI Work Order.

## Constraints

- Preserve Moving's product identity, selected move context, evidence-rich
  capture flow, phone use, and household-first vocabulary.
- A clear directive must be enough; room/belongings context is optional.
- Exact state meaning, activity, permissions, result readability, and truthful
  AI connection status cannot be redesigned away.
- Do not turn Queue into a generic Kanban/task manager or copy another product's
  palette and vocabulary.

## Completion evidence

- `/queue` and compatibility routes have intentional signed-in/signed-out
  behavior and no contradictory Queue vocabulary.
- Loading, empty, denied, error, retry, disconnected/unknown AI, expired claim,
  Needs You, Working, Waiting, Done, long-text, and dense-history states pass.
- Keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion, phone/tablet/desktop, and no-overflow
  browser proof is attached.
- OAuth/API-key scenarios produce results that the person can inspect in the UI.

## Unknown or access limitation

No final UI design has been approved. This is deliberately a separate product
choice and implementation tranche, not a backend defect.

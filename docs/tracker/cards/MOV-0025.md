## Why this exists

The real backend should lead to a clear Moving-specific experience, not force
Claude Design to reverse-engineer placeholder cards or copy another Assist site.

## Current truth

The Queue foundation and product UI are released through protected PR `#177`
at merge SHA `835c696`. The global and move-scoped Queue routes share a
Moving-specific route-note desk, expose exactly Needs You / Working / Waiting
for your AI / Done, combine canonical handoffs with the bounded capture
adapter, and show directive, current step, exact question, readable result,
and attributable activity.

Connection copy reports only verified API-key availability and explicitly says
that AssistWithMoving cannot know whether an AI client is online. Saving a
handoff says it waits for a chosen AI rather than promising an autonomous
runner. Thirteen focused component tests and 45 focused Queue/authority tests
pass on the replacement head. Its full local run passed 1,031 of 1,033 tests;
the two unrelated UI tests that exceeded their timeouts both pass in a 33-test
isolated rerun. Exact-SHA PR CI and post-merge CI run `31641046075` passed both
the Required CI gate and the informational full suite.
Phone and desktop deterministic rendering had no horizontal overflow or
automated accessibility violations. Safe authenticated read-only proof reached
the marked development account and observed 41 synthetic moves with zero
mutations; populated Queue lifecycle proof remains local because another active
lane owns an incompatible synthetic development schema field. Vercel recorded
a successful Production deployment for exact merge SHA `835c696`; the
configured build deployed Convex before building the web app. Signed-out live
proof redirects `/app/queue` to sign-in without exposing Queue data, and the
noncanonical `/queue` route remains an intentional 404.

A later authenticated Vercel-preview pass exercised the released interface with
only removable synthetic data. It proved the immediate loading state, empty
Needs You state, exact four-state navigation, manager/composer owner guard,
canonical directive creation, capture-adapter visibility and specialized
workspace handoff, two AI claims, person input, readable Done result, and the
full attributed activity trail. A move-restricted key could reach its chosen
move and was denied without exposure on a second synthetic move. Both moves and
the key were permanently removed after the pass. This is authenticated preview
proof, not authenticated production proof.

**Priority:** Now. **Change class:** frontend product implementation and browser
verification through Work Order `MOV-WO-004`.

## Next safe action

Use only a production-matched disposable identity and key, with an approved
cleanup path, for later authenticated production and second-identity isolation
proof. Complete a fresh independent audit if that proof becomes safely
available; do not substitute real user data.

## Constraints

- Preserve Moving's product identity, selected move context, evidence-rich
  capture flow, phone use, and household-first vocabulary.
- A clear directive must be enough; room/belongings context is optional.
- Exact state meaning, activity, permissions, result readability, and truthful
  AI connection status cannot be redesigned away.
- Do not turn Queue into a generic Kanban/task manager or copy another product's
  palette and vocabulary.

## Completion evidence

- `/app/queue` redirects signed-out visitors to sign-in, while `/queue` remains
  an intentional 404; neither path exposes private Queue data.
- Loading, empty, denied, error, retry, disconnected/unknown AI, expired claim,
  Needs You, Working, Waiting, Done, long-text, and dense-history states pass.
- Keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion, phone/tablet/desktop, and no-overflow
  browser proof is attached.
- Local API-key/authority scenarios and the marked development read-only auth
  probe pass; production-authenticated mutation/revocation/cleanup remains an
  explicitly unproved layer rather than an inferred release claim.

## Unknown or access limitation

The completed mutation/revocation/cleanup pass used an authenticated protected
preview and two removable synthetic moves. Production-authenticated behavior,
a live second identity/tenant, claim-expiry cron, and route-level injected-error
rendering remain unproved; local authorization and component regressions cover
those paths without turning them into browser claims. Real user data is not a
substitute.

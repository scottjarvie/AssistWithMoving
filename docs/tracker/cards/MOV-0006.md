## Why this exists

Moving is used on phones while people are carrying, packing, photographing,
and coordinating. Small controls create avoidable errors and exclude people
with motor or vision limitations during the product's most important entry
journeys.

## Current truth

Live checks at 360, 768, and 1366 CSS pixels found no horizontal overflow or
application-error marker on `/`, `/ai`, `/faq`, `/updates`, and `/mcp/guide`.
However, many interactive controls measured below the Core 44px floor: the
mobile menu was 28px high, common public CTAs were 32–42px, FAQ summaries were
24px, MCP copy controls were 28px, and live Clerk sign-in actions were 32px.
The issue is systemic across the first-use shell, not an isolated button.

**Priority:** Now. **Change class:** product accessibility change.

## Next safe action

Use a normal UI PR to establish a shared 44px minimum hit-area treatment for
first-use navigation, buttons, disclosure summaries, copy controls, and auth
actions while preserving Moving's compact visual language.

## Constraints

- Measure the clickable hit area, not only visible glyph or text height.
- Preserve keyboard focus, semantic roles, reduced motion, zoom to 200%, and
  no-overflow behavior across phone, tablet, and desktop.
- Do not hide important actions or make dense product workspaces unusably loose.
- UI/component changes require normal PR, CI, browser QA, and deployment.

## Completion evidence

- Automated browser measurements on `/`, `/ai`, `/faq`, `/updates`,
  `/mcp/guide`, `/sign-in`, and `/sign-up` find no essential interactive target
  below 44 by 44 CSS pixels, with documented exceptions only for inline links.
- Keyboard-only traversal shows visible focus and working activation; 200% zoom
  and 360px checks show no horizontal overflow or obscured fixed navigation.
- Representative phone/tablet/desktop screenshots and an accessibility scan are
  attached to the PR, followed by normal build and live verification.

## Unknown or access limitation

Authenticated workspace controls were not measured because this audit did not
use an account. The first implementation should inventory those controls before
claiming the floor is product-wide.

## Why this exists

A green required workflow is useful only when maintainers can reproduce its
meaning locally and distinguish product regressions from a slow or flaky test
harness. Future retrofit work needs a trustworthy baseline.

## Current truth

The one shared-machine failure snapshot was superseded by repeated protected
software runs. Queue, MCP, first-run, and Queue-result releases increased the
suite to 1,050 tests and passed both Required CI and the informational full
suite, most recently on merge `147cff4` in Actions run `31768988479`; the later
tracker freshness main run `31776553297` also passed. No current deterministic
suite failure remains to justify a standalone reliability program.

## Next safe action

Treat any future reproducible failure as a focused product/test defect in the
Work Order that exposes it. Do not revive the old nine-timeout snapshot.

## Constraints

- Do not call a timeout a product failure without an independent reproduction.
- Do not hide a genuine missing-table or interaction defect by retrying it away.
- Preserve accessibility and user-event semantics in component tests.
- Test/config/source changes require a normal PR and full workflow.

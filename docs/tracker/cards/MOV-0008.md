## Why this exists

A person and their chosen AI need one predictable, durable handoff contract
without turning Moving's capture, job, review, export, plan, or sale workflows
into fake Queue states.

## Current truth

The authorized retrofit shipped through protected PR #175 at merge `3566dc8`
with a canonical `queueItems` model,
append-only `queueActivities`, exact four-state invariants, bounded indexed
queries/search, authorization-safe owner pagination, personal ownership/delegation, expiring claims, optimistic
versions, idempotent commands, bounded retry/failure/cancel/expiry semantics,
account-export coverage, and a non-destructive adapter for all six capture
states. Focused tests pass locally. Existing capture storage is intentionally
unchanged and no production backfill was invented.

The preserved source diff was transferred from the exact `0461265` baseline
with matching file manifests and content hashes; no Project Philosophy or
unrelated saved-checkout work entered. Release audit then fixed four Queue
contract defects: optional-expiry index bounds, authorization-before-replay,
expired-lease activity, and strict result/retry bounds. Canonical OAuth Queue
functions remain internal and are deliberately unregistered until a distinct
chosen-AI grant can be enforced; the API-key REST/MCP surface remains the
available agent path.

Exact-SHA main CI, Vercel, and production Convex deployment pass. Live OpenAPI
publishes all seven Queue routes and exactly four states; signed-out `/queue`
remains Moving's intentional 404 and `/app/queue` redirects to Clerk. Protected
PRs `#177`, `#179`, and `#186` later shipped the designed Queue, semantic
authorization/cleanup repairs, and linked canonical-MCP results. Marked
production acceptance proved the ordinary Queue and Move overview, then removed
the synthetic move, Queue, result, client, grant, and session. The foundation is
complete; residual grant and multi-client expansion stays Partial/Later.

## Why this exists

Moving's distinct value is not merely creating an item. It is carrying truthful
records from spaces and photo evidence through packing, transport/capacity,
move day, and the right owner/mover/claims/PCS packet.

## Current truth

Source contains capture, photos, items, boxes, movable units, spaces, plan,
transport, load plan, move day, sell, claims, PCS, employer/mover/load packets,
exports, and share views. Public pages describe many of these as current. The
audit safely verified public routes and representative source, but it did not
create records, upload media, calculate capacity, move an item through states,
or open/export a live packet. Some packet source also says documentation may be
disabled by rollout controls.

**Priority:** Later after the first-move proof. **Change class:** verification
gap; discovered defects become separately scoped product Cards.

## Next safe action

Using a marked fixture household and synthetic media, run one realistic move
from origin/destination spaces through evidence capture, item/box corrections,
load assignment/capacity review, move-day completion, and at least two packet
audiences with deliberate redaction differences.

## Constraints

- Use synthetic/non-sensitive images and values; do not upload personal data.
- Verify calculation assumptions and unknown values rather than inventing
  precision.
- Respect feature flags and label disabled packet types honestly.
- Split unrelated defects; do not turn the proof into an unbounded redesign.

## Completion evidence

- The fixture preserves IDs, quantities, measurements, photo provenance,
  container membership, assignment, status, and history across the lifecycle.
- Capacity math and warnings reconcile to independently calculated fixture
  values with unknown/manual/actual distinctions intact.
- Owner versus mover/claims/PCS outputs show the correct fields, redactions,
  expiry, download/action permissions, and revocation behavior.
- Phone/desktop browser record, exported artifacts, full CI, normal deploy, and
  independent scenario audit pass.

## Unknown or access limitation

No authenticated data, storage upload, generated packet, calculation result, or
public share token was exercised. These flows are source-present but live-unproven.

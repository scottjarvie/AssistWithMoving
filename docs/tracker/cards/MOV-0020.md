## Why this exists

Moving's distinct value is not merely creating an item or saving a loose file.
It is letting a small move stay simple while the same durable record can grow
through household coordination, providers, evidence-heavy paperwork, move day,
post-move claims and later explanation by the owner's chosen AI.

## Current truth

Source contains households/participants, capture, photos, items, boxes, movable
units, spaces, plan, transport resources/zones/trips, load plan, move day, sell,
documentation profiles, claims/PCS/employer/mover/load packet source, exports,
and recipient workflow links. These are strong foundations, not proof of the
approved connected direction: a progressive simple-to-complex setup,
permission-filtered Move Brief, original/versioned artifact memory,
extracted-versus-confirmed facts, completed-form snapshots, requirements
progress, attributed packet history and post-move retrieval. No authenticated
end-to-end path was exercised, and some packet source says documentation may be
disabled by rollout controls.

**Priority:** Later after the first-move proof. **Change class:** product
contract plus verification gap; discovered defects become separately scoped
Cards.

## Next safe action

Define one composable contract and marked-fixture scenario ladder: simple move;
coordinated household; provider-assisted; documented/claim move; and year-later
return. Specify the Move Brief and Document Memory / Move Evidence Packet from
the same authoritative records, then implement in bounded Work Orders only
after current source paths and rollout flags are reverified.

## Constraints

- Use synthetic/non-sensitive images and values; do not upload personal data.
- A small move must not require participant, provider, packet or advanced
  evidence setup before it becomes useful.
- The Move Brief is permission-filtered current context, not a duplicate source
  of truth; sensitive data and expired/revoked grants stay excluded.
- Preserve originals separately from extracted/proposed/person-confirmed facts
  and completed-form snapshots; never silently reuse an old answer as current.
- The owner controls read/write/delete/export/share and external submission
  handoffs. Moving never submits paperwork, acts as a mover, guarantees
  compliance or claims an official legal/military/PCS/provider integration.
- Verify calculation assumptions and unknown values rather than inventing
  precision.
- Respect feature flags and label disabled packet types honestly.
- Split unrelated defects; do not turn the proof into an unbounded redesign.

## Completion evidence

- The same fixture grows from origin/destination, a few boxes and one vehicle to
  household roles, multiple resources/trips, providers and a documented claim
  without duplicate records or mandatory enterprise-first setup.
- An authorized Move Brief returns only permitted current places, timeline,
  roles, inventory, transport, provider context, requirements/evidence, open
  questions, permissions and next Queue work with source/time/actor context.
- Original artifacts retain type, issuer/source, date, version/hash where
  practical and retention/access state; extracted/proposed and
  person-confirmed facts remain visibly distinct.
- A completed-form snapshot preserves what was approved/submitted and later
  corrections; an evidence packet groups the right photos, inventory,
  receipts, forms, source instructions and communication notes.
- Capacity math and warnings reconcile to independently calculated fixture
  values with unknown/manual/actual distinctions intact.
- Owner versus mover/claims/PCS outputs show correct fields, redactions,
  expiry, download/action permissions and revocation without implying official
  integration or product-performed submission.
- A year-later authorized retrieval explains what was filed and why, compares a
  new requirement, identifies missing evidence, and prepares a draft/checklist
  while honoring deletion, retention and revoked access.
- Phone/desktop browser record, exported artifacts, full CI, normal deploy, and
  independent scenario audit pass.

## Unknown or access limitation

No authenticated data, storage upload, generated packet, calculation result,
public share token, official provider connection or post-move retrieval was
exercised. Existing pieces are source-present; the connected direction is
Later / unverified.

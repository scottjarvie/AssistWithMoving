## Why this exists

A discovery response, SDK harness, client registration, or token exchange does
not prove that a person can use an actual AI product through a full private move
workflow. Each named client needs its own evidence before public instructions
call it supported.

## Current truth

Moving has strong official-SDK production evidence: anonymous discovery,
temporary DCR, consent/token exchange, all eight tools, bounded move work,
idempotent save and correction, normal UI reflection, refresh/client/session
cleanup, and fixture purge. It has no complete product lifecycle for Claude,
ChatGPT, Codex, Grok, Hermes, or another named AI product. Private media,
simultaneous-client isolation, reconnect/stale tools, ordinary person-controlled
revocation, and mobile setup remain Partial or Unknown.

## Next safe action

Build one repeatable marked-fixture acceptance runner and evidence matrix, then
exercise one real compatible AI product end to end. Add other intended clients
only when access and product support make a full lifecycle safe; keep every
untested row Unknown and vendor-neutral public copy intact.

## Constraints

- Use only approved non-privileged test identities and clearly marked removable
  synthetic moves, grants, clients, sessions, and records.
- Never inspect or mutate Scott's or another person's move, provider account,
  Queue, evidence, token, or session.
- Do not change Clerk/provider policy, factors, secrets, DNS, billing, or access
  to make a client pass.
- Record desktop/mobile setup availability separately. A mobile client may use
  an existing connection without being able to add one.
- A passed client does not prove another client. SDK proof remains harness proof.
- Stop and record the smallest exact provider/client limitation when the product
  cannot complete a standards-compatible path.

## Completion evidence

- The acceptance matrix records client/product version, platform, registration
  path, endpoint, protocol/tools, scopes, approvals, and evidence for each step.
- At least one real AI product completes discover/connect → consent → tool list →
  bounded read → protected evidence → complete save → follow-up/Queue result →
  normal UI reflection → revoke → immediate refusal → reconnect/stale-tool
  recovery → exact cleanup.
- Cross-owner/move/client, malformed/replayed request, wrong-resource/client,
  expired/revoked grant, idempotency, conflict, and approval-fatigue scenarios
  pass without touching real data.
- Every temporary client, token/grant, session/device, move, Queue item, media,
  planning/source record, and test credential is removed exactly; a re-query
  proves absence and records any intentionally retained test identity.
- Public `/ai`, `/ai.txt`, `/mcp/guide`, release notes, and tracker list exact
  setup steps only for clients with this receipt. Others remain capability-based
  candidates, not supported-client claims.
- A separate AI audits the complete Work Order and records Passed or Follow-up
  needed independently from execution.

## Unknown or access limitation

Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Grok, Hermes, and other conforming clients are intended
clients, but every named product remains Unknown until its own lifecycle passes.

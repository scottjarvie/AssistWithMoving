## Why this exists

A signed-in person needs one calm place to answer “who am I here, what can my
AI access, and what should I do next?” without searching through move-specific
screens or an overloaded settings tab.

## Current truth

Public `/me` returns 404. The account menu exposes Settings, AI Connections,
Admin for admins, and sign-out; Settings groups Overview, People, AI access, and
Privacy. Source also contains account posture, connection counts, keys, grants,
and partial audit/activity data. Those ingredients are not assembled into the
Core `/me` self-status destination, and authenticated behavior was not tested.

**Priority:** Next. **Change class:** product/account information architecture.

## Next safe action

Design `/me` as a small self-status hub that reuses current sources: identity,
selected household/move, active people and AI access, recent meaningful
activity, and direct links to AI, data, privacy, and sign-out controls. Keep
detailed editing in Settings rather than duplicating it.

## Constraints

- Show only the signed-in person's authorized data and safe summaries.
- Do not expose secrets, full private inventory, or admin-only cost data.
- Keep `/me` distinct from `/admin` and move workspaces.
- Route/UI/query/test changes require normal safeguards.

## Completion evidence

- `/me` returns the intended protected page and signed-out users reach sign-in.
- Marked owner/member fixtures see correct identity, access, AI status, recent
  activity, and next links with no cross-user or cross-move leakage.
- Empty/loading/error/revoked states are plain, responsive, keyboard-usable,
  and truthful about unavailable data.
- Full CI, preview, live authenticated proof, and independent review pass.

## Unknown or access limitation

No authenticated account posture or active connection was inspected. Query
cost and the exact safest activity summary must be measured before the Work
Order becomes Ready.

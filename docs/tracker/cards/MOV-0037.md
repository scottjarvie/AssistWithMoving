## Why this exists

A person who revokes an AI connection reasonably expects that AI to lose
access. After `MOV-WO-010`, revoking a grant refuses the very next call on the
canonical `/mcp` door — but it does not touch the legacy `/mcp/connect`
gateway, a scoped `mmk_` API key, or the local stdio package, because those
doors authorize a different way and were deliberately left working.

That is defensible and it is currently disclosed, in `/settings/ai` and in the
agent-facing guides. It is still a gap between what a person means by "revoke"
and what one button does, and it should be closed by a decision rather than
left to documentation forever.

## Current truth

`MOV-WO-010` made a product grant the authority for the canonical door. Only
`convex/mcpPlanning.ts`, `convex/mcpQueueWork.ts`, and `convex/mcpArchive.ts`
consult it. The legacy gateway tools in `convex/mcpTools.ts` and its siblings
authorize through the older persisted OAuth gateway with its own larger
catalog; `/api/mcp` and `mcp-server/` authorize with `mmk_` keys and their own
scopes.

So today a person has three separate revoke surfaces: grants on
`/settings/ai`, API keys in the same screen's fallback section, and nothing
person-facing at all for a legacy `/mcp/connect` connection. The last one is
the real gap — there is no list of legacy connections and no way to end one
without an operator.

Nothing here is a live incident: the legacy door still enforces move access,
participant agent kill-switches, and household permissions. It simply is not
governed by the grant a person now believes governs their AI.

## Next safe action

Decide the intended contract before writing code, because the options differ
in what they break:

1. **Extend grants to the legacy door.** Most honest, and it may break existing
   connections that have no grant — which is exactly the migration `MOV-0032`
   said needs reconnect evidence first.
2. **Give legacy connections their own visible list and revoke control**, kept
   separate from grants. Smaller, and leaves two concepts on screen.
3. **Retire `/mcp/connect`** once migration and reconnect proof exist, which
   removes the question rather than answering it.

Then measure how many real connections use the legacy door. If the answer is
zero, option 3 is much cheaper than it looks.

## Constraints

- Do not silently merge or remove a door. `MOV-WO-010` and `MOV-0032` both
  require migration and reconnect evidence first.
- Do not weaken the canonical door's grant enforcement to make the doors match.
- Keep OAuth connections, API keys, people, and share links impossible to
  confuse, whatever the answer.
- Preserve attribution for work already done through any door.

## Completion evidence

- A recorded decision naming which doors a person-facing revoke reaches, and
  why.
- Either the legacy door enforces grants, or a person can see and end a legacy
  connection, or the door is retired with reconnect proof.
- `/settings/ai` and the agent-facing guides state the resulting contract
  exactly, with no gap between the button's label and its effect.

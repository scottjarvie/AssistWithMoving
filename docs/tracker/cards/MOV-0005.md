## Why this exists

Bring-your-own AI is central to Moving's identity. Setup instructions and tool
claims must be both easy for a person to follow and proven against the hosted
surface before the product treats them as Current.

## Current truth

The original gap is closed. `/ai.txt`, `/ai`, `/ai/start`, and `/mcp/guide`
describe the three separate MCP doors without merging their claims. The
canonical eight-tool OAuth surface completed real production authorization,
brief/search/read, one-call source-backed save, idempotent replay, granular
correction, normal Move and Queue reflection, revocation, and cleanup through
protected PRs `#180`, `#182`, and `#186`. Exact receipts live in `MOV-0028`,
`MOV-0029`, `MOV-0031`, and their Work Orders.

## Next safe action

and named-client compatibility work in `MOV-0032`; do not rerun this completed

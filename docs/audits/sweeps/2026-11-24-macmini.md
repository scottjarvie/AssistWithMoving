## Method and coverage

Fifteen public pages were navigated in incognito, and four signed-out gates including `/admin` redirected to sign-in. Scott's default-profile session was confirmed by the signed-in product shell. Product route patterns were navigated read-only using one move from the list; signed-in `/admin` rendered administrative sections. Some older URLs redirected, as noted below. No forms or write controls were used.

Full navigations used the ordinary browser cache. For `/app/moves`, three desktop navigations used `readiness:none` followed by a passive browser MutationObserver for a rendered move-card link. Browser-clock appearance times were 3146.9, 1164.9 and 1425.3 ms, median 1425.3 ms. The tab reported `visible=false`, and Performance paint entries were empty on those samples. Other routes lack three usable readiness samples; no estimates are substituted. Resource counts and transferred KB are Performance entries after the desktop load, subject to cache and cross-origin visibility limits. Some evaluations caught a previous page during redirects; their metrics are omitted. Console and failed-request counts are null because preview logs are capped and cumulative.

Desktop checks used 1280px. Phone checks used iPhone 12 Pro, confirmed `innerWidth=390`; after settling, document scroll width was checked.
